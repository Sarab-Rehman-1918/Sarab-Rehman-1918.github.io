About

I'm a Computer Engineering student who likes taking a model the whole way — from raw, messy data to something a person can actually use. My work spans deep learning, generative AI, computer vision, and NLP, and I care about the parts that get skipped: careful preprocessing, honest evaluation, and explainability.

My research interest is Brain-Computer Interfaces. For my final-year work I've been building tools that check whether AI-generated brain signals are actually safe to use in clinical settings — because a synthetic EEG can look statistically fine and still be medically wrong.

Across internships at ITSOLERA, Ezitech, and Finetech, I've shipped clinical ML, generative-model benchmarks, and a real-time computer-vision control system.

Ledger Discrepancy Narrator

Code

Compares transaction logs across ERP, Bank, CRM, and Payment systems, deterministically flags mismatches — unmatched, duplicated, mistimed, or partial — and then generates a plain-language, evidence-linked root-cause explanation for each one with an LLM. GENERATIVE AI · CLINICAL · MULTIMODAL

Grounded Multimodal Clinical Report Generator

Code

A research-grade pipeline that generates radiology reports from medical images and patient history — encoding both into a shared embedding space with Swin Transformer and PubMedBERT, retrieving similar prior cases via RAG, fusing image and text with bidirectional cross-attention, then generating a cited report. A three-signal verification filter (textual consistency, visual-classifier agreement, and token-level entropy) redacts any claim it can't confirm, with a full audit log for every prediction.
